I am **Leandro Fusco**, a software developer and infrastructure engineer based in Argentina. Over my career, I've bridged backend engineering with the operational depth of Linux systems and cloud environments.

### Technical Philosophy

- **Simplicity over accidental complexity**: The best systems are those that team members can clearly reason about, deploy, and troubleshoot at 3 AM without surprises.
- **Observability from day zero**: Metrics, structured logs, and distributed tracing are not afterthoughts—they are the bedrock of reliable production software.
- **Native & lightweight tooling**: I favor standalone single binaries (e.g. Go) and minimal container layers with zero bloated dependencies.
- **Pragmatic automation**: Eliminating manual operational toil through robust provisioning, automated testing, and dependable CI/CD pipelines.
