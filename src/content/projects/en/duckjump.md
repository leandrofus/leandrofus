A complete ground-up reimagining of Apache Guacamole: single-binary Go gateway paired with a high-performance Rust session daemon (`duckjumpd`) over a custom DJTP binary protocol. Delivers in-browser remote desktop/terminal access, Just-In-Time expiring grants, automated session recording to S3, and verifiable HMAC audit chains.
