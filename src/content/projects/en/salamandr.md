An omnichannel support platform built for teams who insist on data sovereignty. Features drag-and-drop bot triage flows, local knowledge-base AI answering with Ollama, business-hours SLAs, sandboxed WebAssembly plugins, and a rock-solid containerized Go/Postgres backend.
